# Works Discussed in the Literature Review

This file lists the publications discussed in Section 3 ("Literature Review on Refactoring Towards Microservices") of the article.

## What this list is, and is not

The original literature review (see Section 3.1 of the article) screened, classified, and analyzed 88 publications, later complemented with 5 more during a targeted update (93 total), grouping them into four levels of relevance (33 / 36 / 11 / 8 publications, respectively, before the update). The records of that screening and per-publication classification were not retained and could not be recovered for this package.

What follows instead is a list of 62 works reconstructed from the publications that are actually cited and discussed in the body of Section 3. They are a good proxy for the publications that were analyzed in enough depth to be discussed by name, but they are **not** a reconstruction of the original 88/93-publication corpus or of its relevance classification, because:

- Publications classified at the lower relevance levels (roughly 19 of the original 88) were, by design, not analyzed in depth and are unlikely to be cited individually in the text, so they are largely absent from this list.
- A handful of entries below are supporting or definitional references (e.g., foundational descriptions of microservices, refactoring, or Conway's Law) cited alongside the reviewed literature, rather than works retrieved through the review's search process.

We report this limitation, and its consequences for reproducibility, in the article's "Limitations of the Review" subsection (Section 3.1).
